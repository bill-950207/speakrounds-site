SpeakRounds Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026 · Last reviewed: 16 September 2026

SpeakRounds is an independent OET Nursing Speaking practice application. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OET. This policy explains how the SpeakRounds mobile application and its practice service process information.

1. Who operates SpeakRounds

The data controller is JoCoding, Inc. (Republic of Korea).

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to bill@jocoding.net.

2. Information processed

Information stored on the device

The app stores the learner's onboarding choices, interface language, feedback language, test-planning selections, and limited local practice state. The user can remove this information from Profile & settings by choosing Delete local practice data. Removing the app also removes its local data, subject to normal operating system backup behavior.

Information processed during Live AI practice

When the learner starts a Live AI role-play, SpeakRounds processes:

  • microphone recordings made during the fictional role-play and targeted retries;
  • speech transcripts generated from those recordings;
  • the selected fictional scenario and the conversation turns;
  • generated AI-patient responses and practice feedback;
  • technical metadata such as an anonymous session ID, request ID, audio duration, file size, processing status, token or character usage, and latency.

Learners must not include a real patient's name, contact details, medical record, or other real-world patient information. SpeakRounds is an educational simulation, not a clinical-record system.

SpeakRounds currently has no learner accounts and does not request a legal name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or card details in the app. SpeakRounds Pro purchases are handled by Apple and validated through RevenueCat using an anonymous identifier (see Service providers).

3. How the information is used

The information is used only to:

  • transcribe the learner's practice speech;
  • generate a fictional patient response;
  • produce qualitative, evidence-linked practice feedback;
  • evaluate a targeted retry against the identified communication move;
  • secure, rate-limit, troubleshoot, and measure the reliability of the practice service.

SpeakRounds does not use the recordings or transcripts for advertising, cross-app tracking, data brokerage, or sale. SpeakRounds does not claim that its practice feedback is an official OET result, pass prediction, or clinical advice.

4. Service providers

Live AI practice uses the following processors:

  • xAI receives practice audio for speech-to-text and patient text for text-to-speech.
  • OpenAI receives the transcript, fictional scenario context, and prior fictional conversation turns to generate the AI patient response and practice feedback. SpeakRounds does not send the raw microphone recording to OpenAI.
  • Supabase hosts the encrypted SpeakRounds practice service in Seoul, South Korea (AWS ap-northeast-2).
  • RevenueCat manages in-app purchases: it receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and App Store purchase receipts to validate subscriptions and unlock SpeakRounds Pro. Payment itself is processed by Apple; SpeakRounds never sees card details. Purchase records are kept while needed to provide the entitlement.

Provider API data is not used to train provider models by default unless the operator explicitly opts in. Under the providers' standard API controls, security or abuse-monitoring records may be retained for up to 30 days. Approved zero-data-retention controls may reduce this period. The in-app notice and this policy will be updated if the active provider controls change.

Product analytics, reliability, and attribution use these providers:

  • PostHog (US region) receives product-usage events, such as screens reached, role-plays started or finished, reports opened, and paywall or purchase outcomes. Events use a randomly generated device identifier and may include an opaque practice-session ID to diagnose report delivery. Practice audio, transcripts, report text, and learner sentences are not sent to PostHog. Session replay is disabled.
  • Sentry receives diagnostic information about application errors, crashes, and hangs to help us investigate reliability. Screenshots and default personal-information collection are disabled.
  • Meta receives app activation and attribution information. RevenueCat can share purchase attribution using the Meta SDK's anonymous identifier. On iOS, access to the advertising identifier is subject to App Tracking Transparency permission. Users can decline or change this permission in device settings. Practice recordings, transcripts, and report text are not used for advertising.

Provider policies:

  • OpenAI API data controls: developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data
  • xAI security and zero-data-retention information: docs.x.ai/developers/faq/security

5. Retention and deletion

SpeakRounds encrypts active server-side session state at rest and automatically deletes it 30 minutes after the session was created. This includes the SpeakRounds copy of raw audio, full transcript, AI-patient turns, and targeted retries. Audio playback does not extend that deadline. Temporary mobile recording files are removed after successful upload or when the flow is disposed; the app also cleans up stale temporary files on a later start where supported.

OpenAI and xAI may separately keep security or abuse-monitoring records for up to 30 days under their standard API settings, as described above. SpeakRounds cannot use the local delete button to shorten a processor's mandatory security retention.

For app versions that display the report-recovery notice and obtain consent, completed feedback (including short quoted sentences and patient context in the report) is separately encrypted and retained for up to 7 days from completion. This allows the same installation to retrieve its report after an interruption; it does not keep the full conversation or recordings longer. Retrieval requires the original session token. Reads do not extend the deadline.

Saved reports on the device remain until local practice data is deleted. Deleting local data does not erase the encrypted server recovery copy; it expires automatically, or can be deleted through the authenticated session deletion endpoint while the token is available. There is no cross-device cloud account history.

6. Legal basis

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, Live AI practice is processed at the learner's request to provide the practice service. Security and abuse prevention are processed for the legitimate interest of protecting the service, providers, and learners. Microphone access is requested in context and can be denied; without it, Live AI voice practice cannot run, but the illustrative sample remains available.

7. International transfers

The service providers may process information outside the learner's country. The operator configures appropriate contractual and transfer safeguards where required. The production hosting region is Seoul, South Korea.

8. Security

Production traffic uses HTTPS. Provider credentials remain on the server. Active session data is encrypted at rest, bearer-token protected, rate-limited, excluded from caches, and deleted on a fixed expiry. Logs are designed to exclude raw audio, transcripts, provider keys, and session bearer tokens. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Learner choices and rights

Users can:

  • deny or later revoke microphone permission in device settings;
  • delete local practice data in Profile & settings;
  • wait for automatic deletion (30 minutes for sessions; up to 7 days for completed report recovery); and
  • contact bill@jocoding.net about access, deletion, objection, restriction, or another privacy right that applies in their jurisdiction.

Because the beta has no account, the operator may need a session request ID and time window to locate a still-active session and may be unable to identify data after the fixed deletion deadline.

10. Children

SpeakRounds is intended for healthcare professionals and adult OET candidates. It is not directed to children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their information.

11. Changes

Material changes will be shown in the app or on this page with a new effective date. This public policy always matches the behavior of the released app and production provider settings.
